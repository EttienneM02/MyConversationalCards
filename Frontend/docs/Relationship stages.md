10. Prompt Depth System
Each prompt should have a depth rating.

Level 1 — Light
Easy questions suitable for starting a conversation.

Example:

What's your ideal Saturday?

Level 2 — Meaningful
Questions that reveal personality and preferences.

Example:

What experience has shaped the person you've become?

Level 3 — Deep
Questions involving vulnerability, emotions, relationships, and life perspectives.

Example:

What helps you feel emotionally safe enough to be completely yourself with someone?

11. "Go Deeper" Feature
Every suitable prompt can optionally have a follow-up question.

Example
Main prompt

What's your ideal weekend?

Go Deeper

What does that ideal weekend say about what you value most in your life?

The deeper question should naturally build upon the original question rather than simply becoming more serious.

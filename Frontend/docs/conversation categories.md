7. Conversation Categories
7.1 Getting to Know Each Other
Purpose:

Help partners learn about each other's background, personality, experiences, and everyday life.

Topics
Childhood memories

Family

Siblings

Where they grew up

School

University

Friendships

Social life

Personal history

Important life experiences

Example prompts
What was your childhood like?

What is a memory from growing up that still makes you smile?

Who had the biggest influence on you growing up?

What kind of person were you at school?

What's something about you that people usually discover only after knowing you for a while?

7.2 Fun & Playful
Purpose:

Create laughter, spontaneity, teasing, and low-pressure interaction.

Topics
Funny stories

Embarrassing moments

Hypothetical situations

Childhood mischief

Silly preferences

Games

"Would you rather?" scenarios

Unexpected experiences

Inside jokes

Example prompts
What's the most ridiculous thing you've ever done on a date?

If we had to leave tomorrow for a spontaneous adventure, where would we go?

What's a completely useless talent you have?

What would your funniest reality-TV show be called?

7.3 Interests & Hobbies
Purpose:

Discover what each partner enjoys and what they would like to experience together.

Topics
Music

Movies

Books

Television

Sports

Games

Art

Cooking

Travel

Hobbies

Personal interests

Example prompts
What kind of music can instantly improve your mood?

What's a hobby you've always wanted to try?

What's one movie you could watch repeatedly?

What would your ideal weekend look like?

7.4 Personality & Values
Purpose:

Explore what matters to each partner and how they see the world.

Topics
Happiness

Personal values

Beliefs

Principles

Respect

Honesty

Ambition

Kindness

Personal growth

Admiration

Character

Example prompts
What quality do you admire most in another person?

What does a good life mean to you?

What's something you will never compromise on?

What makes you feel genuinely respected?

What kind of person do you want to become?

7.5 Romance & Attraction
Purpose:

Create romantic connection, affection, chemistry, and playful intimacy.

Topics
Attraction

Affection

Romance

Love languages

Dating experiences

Compliments

Memorable dates

Romantic gestures

Flirting

Emotional connection

Example prompts
What was one of the first things you found attractive about me?

What makes a date feel genuinely romantic to you?

What's your favorite way to receive affection?

What small romantic gesture means a lot to you?

What makes you feel especially connected to someone?

7.6 Relationships
Purpose:

Help partners understand how they approach relationships and partnership.

Topics
Communication

Trust

Loyalty

Boundaries

Conflict

Respect

Affection

Expectations

Partnership

Previous relationship lessons

Example prompts
What makes you feel heard during a disagreement?

What does trust mean to you in a relationship?

How do you prefer to resolve conflict?

What's something every healthy relationship needs?

What have previous relationships taught you about yourself?

7.7 Future & Dreams
Purpose:

Explore aspirations, ambitions, lifestyle preferences, and possible shared futures.

Topics
Career

Ambitions

Travel

Lifestyle

Home

Marriage

Children

Family

Finances

Personal goals

Adventures

Example prompts
Where would you love to live for a few years?

What is something you hope to accomplish in the next five years?

What would your dream life look like?

What kind of adventures would you like to experience with a partner?

What does your ideal future relationship look like?

7.8 Deep & Emotional
Purpose:

Encourage vulnerability and meaningful emotional connection.

These prompts should be presented as optional and should never pressure users into revealing sensitive information.

Topics
Dreams

Fears

Insecurities

Vulnerability

Personal growth

Difficult experiences

Pride

Emotional needs

Feeling safe

Feeling loved

Being understood

Example prompts
What's something you wish people understood about you?

What makes you feel emotionally safe with someone?

What's something you're proud of that you rarely talk about?

What is something you've learned about yourself in the last few years?

When do you feel most loved?

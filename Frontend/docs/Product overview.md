1. Product Overview
Conversational Pieces is a dating discussion generator designed to help romantic partners avoid repetitive small talk and discover interesting things to discuss.

The application presents conversation prompts organized by relationship context, emotional depth, and topic category.

The experience should feel:

Romantic

Warm

Playful

Curious

Non-judgmental

Modern

Easy to use

Suitable for both new and established couples

The application should generate conversation prompts, not function as a dating or matchmaking platform.
